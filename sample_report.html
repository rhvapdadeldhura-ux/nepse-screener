How is Score calculated? (click to expand)
Score is a weighted blend of three layers, each 0–100, click any stock's score to see its own breakdown:
1. Technical (50% weight) — momentum, volume vs. its own average, RSI, MACD. Reflects short-term price/volume behavior.
2. Sentiment (25% weight) — today's market breadth (% of NEPSE advancing) blended with whether this stock's sector is outperforming the overall market today. NEPSE is retail-driven, so sector rotation and day-to-day mood genuinely move prices here.
3. Fundamentals (25% weight, only when available) — P/E (15–30 is typical on NEPSE, above 40 is stretched), EPS, and bonus-share consistency over recent years. Only populated once you run --company SYMBOL for that stock -- until then its weight shifts proportionally onto Technical + Sentiment rather than being guessed.
RSI (0–100) — below 30 = oversold (may bounce), above 70 = overbought (may pull back).
Vol (volume ratio) — above 1.3x = notably more interest than usual; below 0.6x = quiet.
None of this is a prediction or financial advice -- it's a way to prioritize your own research, not a buy signal.
